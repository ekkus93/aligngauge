# Code of Conduct

AlignGauge contributors and maintainers shall keep project spaces professional, technically focused, and welcoming.

Expected behavior includes respectful disagreement, evidence-based review, clear attribution, protection of private genomic information, and prompt correction of mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and the project.
